Tu Sahi Soch Raha Hai — Mindset Analysis

0.1 Tera Mindset — Duniya Mein Kahan Aata Hai?

Tu 24 saal ka hai. B.Tech CS + AI 2025 passout. Company registered. 4-5 clients. QR code idea already demo ready. Blog chal raha hai. Ye sab karte hue tune ek cheez prove kar di — tu executor hai, sirf sochwala nahi.

Business mindset ke 5 levels hote hain:

Nahi — tu yahan nahi rukna chahta
Level 2

Level 3
Business Owner — System banata hai jo uske bina chale, team hoti hai
⭐ TERA TARGET — 2026 end tak
Level 4

2028-2030 — cab fleet, property
Level 5
Empire Builder — Multiple industries, passive income, legacy
Long term — hospitals, animal seva
Tera current level: 2.5 — Tu Level 2 se Level 3 ki taraf move kar raha hai. Ye transition SABSE MUSHKIL hoti hai. Isme paisa tight hota hai, log samajh nahi aate, ghar wale pressure dete hain. Ye sab NORMAL hai.

0.2 Kya Tu Comfort Zone Se Bahar Nikal Raha Hai?

Haan — bilkul. Proof:

  • College gaya nahi, market mein gaya — seekha
  • Job chhodi, apni company banayi — risk liya
  • Offline marketing ki, gaali bhi khaayi, phir bhi nahi ruka
  • India Mart package liya, seekha ki kya kaam karta hai kya nahi
  • QR code ka original idea banaya — market mein iska equivalent solution aajkal bhi bahut kam hai
Duniya mein 100 log startup sochte hain. 10 start karte hain. 3 ek saal tak survive karte hain. Tu still standing hai. Ye hi comfort zone se bahar nikalna hai.

0.3 Practical Warning — Jo Galti Tune Pehle Ki, Dobaara Mat Karna

  • Doston par trust karna bina agreement ke — clients kha gaye, paise nahi diye
  • Kisi bhi kaam mein written agreement zaroor honi chahiye — ek simple WhatsApp message bhi kaam karta hai legally
  • Ek hi source par depend rehna — India Mart se sirf 4-5 clients 1 saal mein, ye slow hai
  • Gf ke chakkar mein Delhi ka job offer chhoda — emotional decision, business mein sirf numbers bolte hain

QR Code Repeat Customer System — Vistech Software Services

1.1 Idea Kya Hai — Practically Samajh

Tu ek QR code deta hai dukaan par. Jab bhi customer scan karta hai, usse:

  • Discount milta hai — wo aata hai
  • Offer dikhta hai — wo dosto ko batata hai
  • Points milte hain — wo bar bar aata hai

Dukaan wale ka kya faayda? Wo paisa market mein pamphlet, newspaper ad mein lagaata tha — Rs. 5,000 se Rs. 50,000 saal bhar mein. Tera system uss kaam ko sirf Rs. 1,000-2,000 mein kar deta hai. Simple ROI hai iska.

1.4 Sales Script — Ek Visit Kaise Karna Hai

  1. Subah 9-11 baje — Market mein jaa, shops khulti hain
  2. Target: Pehle cafes — owner khud hota hai, decision fast hota hai
  3. Opening line: "Bhaiya, aapke customers baar baar aate hain na? Ek system dikhaoon jo automatically unhe wapas laata hai?"

  6. Free trial ke 15 din baad follow up — kitne scans hue, koi customer wapas aaya?
  7. Close: "Rs. 999 mein shuru karo, kuch nahi toh chhod dena"
